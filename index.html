Scoring: group win 3 · draw 1 · advance to KO +4 · each KO win +5 · winning the final +10 (5 win + 5 champion bonus). Third-place match is not scored. A gold +N = unconfirmed points: a knockout berth a team has mathematically clinched (the +4, via FIFA 2026 head-to-head-first tie-breakers) but that isn't banked yet because the bracket isn't seeded — it converts to banked points once the group stage resolves. Best-third-place clinches aren't pre-counted, and standings rank by banked points only. "Proj" = expected final total from Silver Bulletin's odds, which he republishes nightly after each matchday (banked points are included in his updated numbers). "vs draft" compares that to the pre-tournament expectation — whose draft is aging well. If the odds feed is unreachable the page falls back to the last fetched copy, then to pre-tournament numbers (see the header note for which is in use).
